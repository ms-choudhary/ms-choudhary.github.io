Mutual Fund Taxation
Posted on 2026-08-19 :: Updated on 2026-08-22:: money

Equity

Must have minimum 65% equity investment for it to be applicable for equity taxation.

  • < 1 yr - STCG - 20%
  • > 1 yr - LTCG - 12.5% - upto 1.25 Lakh exempt per annum

Non Equity

Debt, Gold ETF etc, are taxed on income slab rates.

FIFO Withdrawal

In case of SIPs, oldest lots are redeemed first incase of withdrawal. Each lot is calculated separately for taxes.
